Our story

Three generations of Hamilton car care

Charlie (Chas) McPhail was Ontario’s first certified mechanic and opened Main Garage and Motor Sales in the 1930s at the corner of Tuxedo and Main Streets here in Hamilton. Making his customers happy made him happy.

McPhail Auto was established in 1993 by brothers Scott and Steve McPhail, Charlie’s grandsons, who share his passion for all things mechanical. As a small, family-run business, customer trust has become the backbone of the shop’s success.

When he’s not at the shop, Scott can often be found in the winner’s circles of the Canadian dirt track racing circuit in lucky Number 07, built and maintained by McPhail Automotive.
